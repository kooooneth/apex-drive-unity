Apex Drive

Build the car, pick the driver and take the wheel yourself: cockpit view, no autopilot. Race circuits, run a career, pit for tyres and fight the weather.

Play in your browser Get the Unity version

Browser version

The original, nothing to install. Works on a desktop browser with a keyboard.

  • Garage with a parts shop, livery and race setup
  • Four circuits, plus a track editor for your own
  • Career, tournament, qualifying, achievements
  • Pit stops, DRS, slipstream, weather, safety car
  • Three drivers that level up as you race
WS throttle / brake   AD steer   E engine mode   V camera   Q DRS   R recover   P pause

Unity version

The full game, built for Unity 6 and much bigger. It needs the Unity editor, so it runs from the downloaded project, not in the browser.

  • Six circuits and a different car for each
  • Online multiplayer with a room code
  • Setup lab and tyre strategy planner
  • Fuel load, tyre graining, wind and the real sun
  • DRS zones, rewind, camera views, replay
  • A race engineer who remembers if you listen
  • Pit crews, reliability, facilities, sponsors
